Why I am running

Renewing trust by putting people back at the center of government.

Public service should be understandable, accountable, compassionate, and useful in the daily lives of the people it serves.

A practical movement for national renewal

America does not need more noise. It needs steady leadership, honest information, responsible stewardship, and solutions that reach families, workers, seniors, veterans, students, small businesses, and communities too often left unheard.

Jennifer Pearl 2028 is a campaign for transparent government, affordable living, environmental restoration, ethical technology, accessible education, healthier communities, and a nation capable of solving problems together.

“Leadership is not a performance. It is the patient work of listening, building, repairing, and making sure every person can understand the government that serves them.”
Jennifer Kay Pearl

Meet Jennifer

Artist. Entrepreneur. Builder. Mother. Citizen.

Jennifer Kay Pearl is an artist, entrepreneur, educator, nonprofit founder, environmental advocate, and creator of public digital resources designed to make knowledge and opportunity more accessible.

Her work grows from a belief that people should not have to navigate public life alone. Through education, legal resources, health information, environmental service, creative platforms, and civic tools, she has worked to build practical pathways that help people understand their choices and participate in their communities.

A government people can understand

Public information should belong to the public.

This campaign supports a new standard for accessible, accountable, and understandable government.

Plain-language government

Laws, budgets, agencies, and programs should be written so ordinary people can understand them.

Open public records

Records, contracts, expenditures, and progress reports should be easier to find and compare.

Measurable commitments

Policy promises should include goals, timelines, responsible agencies, and public reporting.

Citizen participation

People should have accessible ways to contribute ideas, submit concerns, and follow decisions.
